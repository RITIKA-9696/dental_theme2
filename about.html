Our Story

The studio behind the quiet craft.

Founded in 2012 by Dr. Amara Sokolova, Aurea has become a reference point for considered dentistry — a practice built on time, taste, and trust.

A Quiet Beginning

It started with a single chair and a simple conviction.

"Dentistry, at its best, is a craft — not a conveyor belt."

Dr. Amara Sokolova opened Aurea in 2012 with just one dental chair, one assistant, and a promise: never more than six patients a day. That single decision shaped everything — the pace, the materials, the way we speak to people, even the way the studio is lit.

Twelve years later, we are still small by choice. We work with a tight, hand-picked team of specialists. We take on a limited number of cases each year. And we still keep the same rule as day one — you are never rushed.

Our patients come for cosmetic work, restorative work, and preventive care, but they stay for something harder to describe: the quiet confidence that comes from being genuinely looked after.

Our Principles

What we hold onto, every single day.

i.

Time is the treatment

Longer sessions, fewer patients per day — good work simply cannot be rushed, so we don't try.

ii.

Materials we believe in

Hand-finished ceramics, premium implants, and only what we would choose for ourselves.

iii.

Honesty, always

If a treatment isn't right for you, we'll say so. If you don't need it, we'll tell you that too.

iv.

Quiet over loud

No marketing noise, no unnecessary upselling — just work we're proud to sign our name to.

v.

One studio, one team

Every case is handled by the same small team — from your first visit to your final review.

vi.

Care that lasts

We plan treatments for the next fifteen years, not the next fifteen minutes.
