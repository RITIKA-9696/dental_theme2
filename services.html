Common Questions

Things patients often ask.

A few answers before your first visit — and if your question isn't here, we're always happy to talk it through.

Ask Us Anything
Which treatment should I start with?

Almost always, a consultation. We'll examine, listen, and recommend a plan in order of priority — sometimes that means starting with something simple and preventive before cosmetic work.

Do you treat nervous patients?

Very much so. A large part of our practice is patients who haven't been to a dentist in years. We work at your pace, with longer appointments and gentle techniques.

How long does a smile design take?

Most cases take 4–8 visits spread over 2–4 months. Complex cases involving implants or orthodontics can take longer — we'll give you a clear timeline at the planning stage.

Are implants painful?

The procedure itself is completely painless under local anaesthesia. Post-operative discomfort is typically mild and well-managed with simple pain relief for a few days.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes — for larger treatment plans we offer staged payments and interest-free instalments. Ask during your consultation and we'll outline the options.

Do you see children?

Aurea is an adult-focused practice, though we do accept teenagers for orthodontics. For younger children, we're happy to recommend trusted pediatric colleagues nearby.
